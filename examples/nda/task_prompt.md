# NDA Task Prompt: Multipair Muon Decay Multiplicity Frontier

The following prompt was used to produce the worked example in `transcripts/`.

---

Determine the largest number of $e^+e^-$ pairs $n$ for which the SM decay
$\mu^+ \to \bar{\nu}_\mu \nu_e + n(e^+e^-) + e^+$ remains observable at
current or planned muon experiments. Begin by establishing the experimental
landscape: existing measurements, planned experiments (Mu3e, etc.), and their
sensitivity reaches. For each multiplicity, enumerate the contributing
tree-level diagrams and estimate the branching ratio using NDA. Continue
increasing $n$ until the rate falls below experimental sensitivity.

Cross-check the dominant diagram class at the lowest and next-to-lowest
non-trivial multiplicity against an exact MadGraph calculation, using coupling
order restrictions to isolate the relevant diagrams. Provide a brief analytical
estimate of the per-pair suppression factor and compare it with the observed
numerical scaling.

Deliverable: a comprehensive table of diagram counts, NDA widths, scalings,
and branching ratios for each final-state multiplicity, identifying the
experimental frontier. Include "diagram metadata" figures that make the
enumeration and NDA results intuitive. Additionally create a summary figure
illustrating how the total branching ratio scales with $n$ relative to
experimental sensitivity thresholds. Conclude with a discussion of the physics
driving the multiplicity scaling and its implications for current and planned
experiments.
