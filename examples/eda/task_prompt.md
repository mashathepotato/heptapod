# EDA Task Prompt: Systematic Decay Width Catalog

The following prompt was used to produce the worked example in `transcripts/`.

---

Enumerate and compute the symbolic partial decay width $\Gamma(A \to BC)$ for
every tree-level, Lorentz-invariant, single-vertex $1 \to 2$ process in four
spacetime dimensions. Systematically sweep over parent spins $\{0, 1/2, 1\}$
and all kinematically allowed daughter spin assignments. Retain complex
couplings and distinct daughter masses throughout; equal-mass, massless, and
real-coupling limits are taken as special cases. For each vertex type that
admits both a vector-axial $(g_V, g_A)$ or scalar-pseudoscalar $(g_S, g_P)$
parameterization and a chiral $(g_L, g_R)$ parameterization, compute both in
parallel and verify algebraic consistency under the basis change.

Validate a representative subset of the computed formulas against known
Standard Model partial widths, using known masses and couplings.

Deliverable: a complete reference table of independent decay-width formulas in
both coupling bases where applicable, together with useful limits, chiral
cross-checks, NDA cross-checks, and Standard Model validation. Include figures
that make the physical content of the catalog accessible and intuitive -- the
key patterns and threshold structure should be assessable from the figures
alone. Finally, provide a summary of phenomenological insights from the
analysis.
